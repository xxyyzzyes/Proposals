Our Story

Born in Taiwan,
grown from the land

Sing Hi (馨海) started with a single question: what if the things we discard every day could come from the earth, and go back to it just as easily? We began with tea fibre straws and never stopped asking. Today, our full range of plant-based essentials proves that living green doesn't mean giving anything up.

01
Agricultural by-products only
We never grow crops for products. We use what the harvest leaves behind.
02
Zero additives, zero coatings
No plastic lining, no binders, no hidden chemicals. Just plant fibre.
03
Taiwan-made, globally trusted
Every product is manufactured and certified in Taiwan, exported worldwide.
